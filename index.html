Who pays for fuel and FASTag?▾
The guest pays for fuel and must return the car with the same fuel level as at pickup. FASTag recharges during the trip are also the guest's responsibility.
What is the refundable security deposit and why do I pay it?▾
The security deposit covers any damage, fines, or shortfall in fuel/cleaning charges. If the car is returned on time and in good condition, it's fully refunded within 5–7 business days.
Can I modify or extend my trip after booking?▾
Yes — message us on WhatsApp before your drop time and we'll try to extend the booking subject to availability. Extension charges apply per hour as per the car's hourly rate.
How do I cancel my booking?▾
Go to "My Bookings" in your profile and cancel from there, or message us on WhatsApp. Refunds follow the cancellation policy shown in the Cancellation tab.
Is doorstep delivery available for this car?▾
Yes — enable "Doorstep Delivery" on the homepage before searching, choose your location, and the delivery charge will be added to your total automatically.
